# V6.2.0 — Trust, brand precision & digital signature

## Changes
### §01 + §14 — Legal identity audit (permanent regression test)
`tests/legal-identity.test.ts`: fails the build if LLP, SREE INNOVATIONS, RKLS, old suffixes or the wrong CIN
appears in any HTML, JSON, XML or manifest file. Negative proofs: injecting "LLP" → FAIL; injecting a wrong CIN →
FAIL; clean → PASS. Also checks: legal name on homepage + Organization schema, correct CIN everywhere, registered
address in every page's footer, no mixed-case legal name.

### §02 — Authoritative footer
Full registered address on separate lines (building, street, locality, region), CIN in mono, incorporation date,
email repeated under "Connect" alongside LinkedIn. Quiet corporate credibility.

### §12 — Proof layer
A verified-facts bar above the copyright line: incorporated company + CIN, registered office, live EduLynx product,
real office and infrastructure, direct contact. Links to the relevant pages. No invented awards, customers or stats.

### §10 — Contact experience
Heading: "Start a conversation." Intent types: Build a product · AI / ML system · School ERP · Software engineering ·
Partnership · General enquiry (was: Product / AI system / Enterprise software / SaaS platform / EduLynx demo / Cloud /
Other). Form fields, validation and backend unchanged.

### §13 — SEO identity
Checked: homepage title contains the legal name; Organization schema `legalName` is correct; no mixed-case variant
anywhere. All pass.
