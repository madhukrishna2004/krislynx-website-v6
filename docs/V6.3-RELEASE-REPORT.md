# V6.3.0 — EduLynx flagship + product experience transformation

## 1. Executive summary
Product experience release. EduLynx remains the flagship with its existing interactive interface, module explorer
and school-day experience (all established in earlier versions). V6.3 adds: a product constellation (SVG connecting
lines), an EduLynx→Engineering bridge, a screenshot-ready asset directory, concept products out of structured data,
and six new permanent tests. No visual redesign; no new dependencies; no architecture changes.

## 2–3. EduLynx / product changes
- **Product constellation (§9):** subtle dashed SVG lines behind the product grid connect a "KRISLYNX" label to the
  flagship and through it to the secondary products. Hidden on phones (too small to read).
- **EduLynx→Engineering bridge (§15):** "From product requirements to working software" with a link to /services,
  bridging the product and engineering narratives.
- **Structured data:** only `status: "live"` products (EduLynx ERP) appear in the schema's ItemList. Concept product
  names are also absent from the page description. The existing `honesty without labels` test now passes.
- **Screenshot-ready (§12):** `public/images/products/edulynx/` directory exists; when real screenshots are added,
  the layout accepts them without a rebuild.
- **Contact intent types (carried from V6.2):** Build a product · AI / ML system · School ERP · Software engineering
  · Partnership · General enquiry.
